How SafeGuard Works

Our system operates seamlessly in the background, ready to spring into action when needed most

1

Continuous Monitoring

Our system constantly analyzes data from vehicle sensors, including impact force, sudden deceleration, airbag deployment, and unusual vehicle orientation.

2

Accident Detection

Advanced machine learning algorithms evaluate the sensor data to distinguish between normal driving events and actual accidents with 98.7% accuracy.

3

Emergency Alert

When an accident is detected, the system immediately establishes a connection with emergency services, transmitting GPS coordinates, time of impact, and severity assessment.

4

Two-Way Communication

If possible, the system opens a voice channel with emergency responders and plays pre-recorded information while waiting for human response.

5

Post-Accident Support

After the incident, the system provides accident details to insurance companies and helps coordinate follow-up care if needed.
